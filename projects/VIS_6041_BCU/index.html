01 / Overview

SLOWER,
MORE
MEANINGFUL.

RITUAL is a slow-living lifestyle brand that turns everyday routines into meaningful, sustainable experiences. Drinking, resting, gathering and gifting become intentional moments that benefit people and the planet.

Its central idea is simple: make more of less.

Role

Brand strategy, identity and delivery.

Audience

Design-aware adults aged 20–35 who care about wellbeing, sustainability and lifestyle culture.

Tone

Calm, warm, intentional and encouraging.

02 / Brand strategy

JOY WITHOUT
THE WASTE.

RITUAL makes lower-impact living feel beautiful rather than restrictive. Thoughtful products, refill systems and calm storytelling frame sustainable habits as emotionally rewarding.

Values
  • Intentional living
  • Sustainability
  • Emotional wellbeing
  • Community and connection
03 / Delivery

A SYSTEM
IN THE
WORLD.

The project maps the identity through refillable lifestyle-product packaging, a “Slow Down. Feel More.” launch campaign, and a digital product supporting rituals, refills and sustainable habits.
