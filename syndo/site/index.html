A place that feels like yours

Make a server for your friend group, your club or your whole community. Sort conversations into channels, set a status so everyone knows what you're up to, and make it yours with emotes, stickers and themes.

Hop in whenever you're free

Voice channels are always open — drop in when you have a minute, no calling round required. Turn on your camera, watch a friend's stream, arrange everyone's boxes how you like, or pop the whole call out into its own window.

Go live in one click

Pick a window or your whole screen right inside SEND and you're streaming. Share a game and only the game's sound goes with it; share your screen and everyone hears it except your call — so there's no echo.

Live window previews and per-app sound are in the SEND app for Windows. Browsers use their own picker.

Straight about safety

Everything you send travels encrypted between your device and SEND. Server owners get roles, permissions, automatic moderation and audit logs to keep their space friendly. We tell you plainly what SEND does and doesn't do.

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS) for messages and calls
  • Roles, permissions and AutoMod for every server
  • Blocking and privacy controls for everyone
  • No ads in your chats
