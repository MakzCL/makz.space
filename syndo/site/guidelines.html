Community Guidelines

Last updated: 13 November 2026

SEND is a place to talk, play and share with your friends and communities. These guidelines apply to everyone, everywhere on SEND: profiles, servers, direct messages, voice, video and streams. They form part of our Terms of Service. Server moderators can add stricter rules for their own servers.

Keep people safe

  • Child safety comes first. Never share, request or create sexual content involving anyone under 18, or sexualise minors in any way, including drawings and AI-generated images. Do not groom, or try to get personal information or images from, anyone under 18. We remove this content, ban the accounts and report it to the authorities.
  • No threats or violence. Do not threaten, encourage or glorify violence against anyone, organise harm, or share content that celebrates terrorism or violent extremism.
  • No harassment. Do not bully, intimidate, stalk or repeatedly contact someone who does not want it, and do not encourage others to pile on.
  • No hate. Do not attack people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age or other protected characteristics.
  • Self-harm. Do not encourage or give instructions for suicide, self-harm or eating disorders. Talking about your own experiences and supporting others is welcome. If you are struggling, please reach out (see Safety).
  • Privacy. Do not share other people's private information (addresses, phone numbers, documents, private photos) without their permission, and never share intimate images of anyone without consent.

Keep it real and fair

  • No spam or scams. No unsolicited mass messages, phishing, fake giveaways, pyramid schemes or attempts to steal accounts.
  • No impersonation. Do not pretend to be another person, brand, SEND staff or MAKZ Labs.
  • Respect creators. Only share content you have the right to share. See our Copyright Policy.
  • No illegal goods or services. Do not buy, sell or promote drugs, weapons, stolen data, counterfeit goods or other illegal things.
  • Play fair with SEND. Do not spread malware, exploit bugs, use bots to abuse the service, get around bans, or buy, sell or trade accounts.

Sexual content

Sexually explicit content is not allowed on SEND. Suggestive content must not be shared with anyone who has not chosen to see it.

What happens if someone breaks the rules

We look at reports and context. Depending on how serious it is, we may remove content, warn, restrict features, or suspend or permanently ban accounts and servers. Serious harm, especially to children, leads to an immediate ban and a report to the authorities. You can appeal any decision through our complaints process in the Terms.

How to report

Right-click or long-press a message, person or server and choose Report, or email send@makz.space. You can also block anyone at any time. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Talk together. Belong together.
