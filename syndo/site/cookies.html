Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar browser feature. This policy explains how SEND, provided by MAKZ Labs, uses them.

Only what SEND needs to work

SEND uses only strictly necessary cookies and storage. Under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and similar laws, these do not need your consent, because SEND cannot work without them. We do not use advertising, tracking, analytics or social media cookies, and we do not let third parties set cookies through SEND.

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
Session cookieCookie (secure, HTTP-only)Keeps you signed in and protects your account from forged requestsUntil you sign out, or up to 30 days
Appearance and app settingsLocal storageRemembers your theme, layout, sound volume, call layout and similar choices on this deviceUntil you clear it
Encryption keysBrowser storage (IndexedDB)Holds the keys for your end-to-end encrypted direct messages on this device. They never leave your device unencryptedUntil you sign out or clear it
Offline filesService worker cacheLets SEND load quickly and work when your connection dropsUntil updated or cleared

Services you choose to use

When you search GIFs or view community emotes, images load from GIPHY, 7TV or BetterTTV. Those services may set or read their own cookies under their own policies. Opening a link takes you to another website with its own cookie policy.

Cookie settings and your choices

Because SEND only uses strictly necessary cookies, there is nothing optional to switch on or off, so we do not show a cookie banner. You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time, but SEND will sign you out and may not work properly, and you may lose the encryption keys stored on that device. If we ever add optional cookies, we will ask for your consent first and add controls here.
