About SEND

SEND is a free place for friends and communities to chat, hop into voice, go live with their screens and just hang out.

Every feature, for everyone

HD screen sharing, custom themes, animated profile effects and decorations, emoji, stickers and noise suppression are free for everyone. We think the good stuff should not be locked behind a subscription.

Private where it matters

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. There are no ads in your chats, and we do not sell your data. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what we collect and why.
