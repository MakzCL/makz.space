Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot your password?" and we will email you a reset link. Resetting signs you out everywhere.

I lost my two-factor device

Sign in with one of your recovery codes. If you have none, email us from the address on your account and we will help you verify that it is yours.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings, My account, then Delete account. This is permanent. Messages you sent stay visible to the people you sent them to, shown as from "Deleted user", so delete any you want removed first.

How do I download my data?

Open Settings, My account and choose to download a copy of your data.

How do I report someone?

Right-click or long-press their message, profile or server and choose Report. See Safety for more.

Windows says the installer is from an unknown publisher

The SEND installer for Windows always comes from send.makz.space or the official download link in the app. If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, choose "More info", check the file name is SEND-Setup, then "Run anyway". We are working on signing the installer so this goes away.

Calls cut out or nobody can hear me

Check SEND has microphone permission, pick the right input in Settings, Voice and video, and try turning Noise suppression off and on. On restrictive networks, try the Worldwide voice network option on the voice channel.
