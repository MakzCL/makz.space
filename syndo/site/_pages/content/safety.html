Safety

SEND is built so you decide who can reach you and what you see. Here is how it keeps you safe, and how to get help.

Report anything

Right-click or long-press a message, person or server and choose Report. Reports go to the SEND safety team at {{COMPANY}}, never to the person you report.

Block and mute

Block someone and they cannot message or call you, and they are not told. Mute servers, channels and conversations whenever you need quiet.

Private messages, actually private

Direct messages and group chats are end-to-end encrypted. Not even {{COMPANY}} can read them.

Protect your account

Turn on two-factor authentication or use a passkey, check your signed-in devices, and never share your password or recovery codes.

Tools for moderators

Roles and permissions, AutoMod keyword and spam filters, timeouts, bans and an audit log help server owners keep their spaces friendly.

Your data, your call

Download your data or delete your account at any time in Settings, My account.

Being honest about encryption

Direct messages and group direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. Server channels and calls are encrypted in transit but are not end-to-end encrypted, so that communities can moderate and newcomers can read history. We never record calls. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Parents and guardians

SEND is for people aged 13 and over. Talk with your teen about who they chat with, show them how to block and report, and help them set up their privacy settings. If you are worried about a child's account, email {{EMAIL}}.

If you need help now

  • Emergency: if anyone is in immediate danger, call 999 in the UK (112 in the EU, 911 in the US) or your local emergency number.
  • Samaritans (UK and Ireland): call 116 123, free, any time, or visit samaritans.org.
  • Childline (UK, under 19): call 0800 1111 or visit childline.org.uk.
  • Report child sexual abuse online: the Internet Watch Foundation at report.iwf.org.uk, or CEOP at ceop.police.uk.
  • Outside the UK: find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com.
