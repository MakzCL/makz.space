Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data SEND collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the rights you have. We have written it to be read, not skimmed past. The short version: we collect what we need to run SEND, we do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and your direct messages are end-to-end encrypted so we cannot read them.

Who we are

SEND is provided by MAKZ Labs, Office 1, Izabella House, 24-26 Regent Place, Birmingham, B1 3NJ, United Kingdom. MAKZ Labs is the data controller for personal data processed through SEND, under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and, where it applies to you, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR). Contact us about privacy at send@makz.space.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, username, display name, password (stored only as a secure hash), and optionally pronouns, an avatar, a banner, a bio, a custom status and profile effects.
  • Security details: two-factor authentication settings, passkeys and recovery codes (stored in protected form).
  • Your content: messages, files, images, voice messages, reactions, polls, emoji, stickers and sounds you upload, servers and channels you create, and the settings you choose. Direct messages and group direct messages are end-to-end encrypted (see section 3).
  • Connections: if you link another service to your profile, the account name or ID that service gives us.
  • Reports and support: what you tell us when you report something or contact support, including any content you choose to include.

Information created when you use SEND

  • Sessions and devices: for each signed-in device, the IP address, device or browser type (user agent) and when it was last used, so you can see and sign out your devices and so we can protect your account.
  • Activity needed to run the service: which servers and channels you belong to, what you have read, your friends and blocks, presence (online, idle, do not disturb) and voice state (which call you are in, muted or not).
  • Voice, video and screen sharing: audio and video travel through our real-time media network to the other people in the call. We do not record calls. Media is encrypted in transit but is not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, a push token or subscription address for your device.
  • Server insights: server owners can see aggregated, non-identifying statistics (for example, how many members were active on a day).
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps short-lived request logs (including IP addresses) for security, abuse prevention and fixing faults.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or biometric data. Camera, microphone and screen access are used only while you choose to use them in a call.

3. Encryption

  • Direct messages and group direct messages, including files sent in them, are end-to-end encrypted on your device. The keys stay on your devices, so MAKZ Labs cannot read them. We still see who you message and when (metadata), because we need that to deliver messages.
  • Server channels are encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and stored on our servers, so that members who join later can read the history and moderators can enforce rules. They are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Calls are encrypted in transit between you and our media network. They are not end-to-end encrypted, and we do not record them.

4. Who we share it with

Other people on SEND see what you choose to share: your profile, messages in the places you post them, and your presence and voice state where your privacy settings allow it.

Service providers process data for us under contracts that require them to protect it and only use it on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, content delivery, file storage, real-time voice and video relay, and protection against attacks.
  • Supabase, Inc.: our database, hosted in London, United Kingdom.
  • Our email delivery provider: sending account emails.
  • Push notification services run by your device or browser maker (for example, Google, Apple, Microsoft or Mozilla): delivering notifications. The content of encrypted messages is not included.

Services you choose to use inside SEND receive some data directly from your device when you use them:

  • GIF search (GIPHY): your search terms are sent to GIPHY through our servers, and the GIFs then load from GIPHY's servers, which see your IP address.
  • Community emotes (7TV and BetterTTV): emote images load from their servers, which see your IP address.
  • Link previews: when a link is posted in a server channel, our servers fetch the page to show a preview; the website sees our server, not you. Opening a link takes you to that website under its own privacy policy.
  • Stripe (payments): if you sponsor SEND or buy an optional extra on the web, Stripe, Inc. and its group companies process your payment. They receive your card details, billing name, email and country directly; we only store a Stripe customer reference, the amount and the status of your sponsorship. See stripe.com/privacy.
  • App stores and payments: if you download SEND from an app store or buy something through one, that store processes your data under its own policy.

Legal reasons. We may disclose data if we believe in good faith it is required by law or a valid legal process, needed to protect someone from death or serious harm, needed to investigate fraud or security issues, or needed to protect the rights, property or safety of MAKZ Labs, our users or the public. Where the law requires us to report child sexual abuse material or other serious crimes, we do. We cannot provide the content of end-to-end encrypted messages because we do not have it.

Business changes. If MAKZ Labs is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may transfer as part of that, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

5. International transfers

Our database is in the United Kingdom. Some providers, such as Cloudflare, operate global networks and may process data in other countries, including the United States, to deliver SEND quickly wherever you are. When personal data leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on adequacy regulations (such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or on approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Contact us for more detail.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, sessions, settings and personal identifiers. Messages you posted in servers and conversations remain visible to the people you sent them to, shown as from "Deleted user", because they are also part of other people's conversations. You can delete your own messages before deleting your account.
  • Messages and files you delete are removed from SEND straight away and from backups within our backup cycle.
  • Sessions are removed when you sign out of a device or when they expire.
  • Payment records (amount, date and Stripe reference of each sponsorship payment) are kept for six years, because UK tax law requires it, even if you delete your account.
  • Technical logs are kept only briefly, normally no longer than 30 days, unless needed to investigate a specific security incident.
  • Reports and related moderation records are kept for as long as needed to keep SEND safe, deal with appeals and meet legal duties.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to: access your data and get a copy; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; receive it in a portable format; withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent; and complain to a data protection authority. Many of these you can do yourself in SEND:

  • edit your profile and settings at any time;
  • download a copy of your data in Settings, My account;
  • see and sign out your devices;
  • delete your account in Settings, My account.

For anything else, email send@makz.space. We will respond within one month and may need to confirm your identity first. If you are in California or another US state with privacy laws, you have similar rights to know, delete and correct your data, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

8. Children

SEND is not for children under 13 (or the higher minimum age where you live). If we learn that we hold data from a child below the minimum age without the required consent, we will delete the account. If you think a child is using SEND without permission, please contact us.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, end-to-end encryption for direct messages, hashed passwords, optional two-factor authentication and passkeys, access controls and monitoring. No service can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and turn on two-factor authentication. If a breach is likely to put your rights at high risk, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

10. Cookies and local storage

We use only the cookies and local storage needed for SEND to work, such as keeping you signed in and remembering your settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when SEND changes or the law requires it. If changes are significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact and complaints

Email send@makz.space or write to MAKZ Labs, Office 1, Izabella House, 24-26 Regent Place, Birmingham, B1 3NJ, United Kingdom. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority where you live. We would appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.
