# SEND brand kit (MAKZ Labs)

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| SEND Blue | `#3D6BFF` | The S, links, primary accents |
| Ink | `#0E1220` | App icon background, text on light backgrounds |
| Night | `#0B1020` | App and website background |
| Sponsor Pink | `#FF5C8A` | Sponsor, Stripe brand colour, buttons |
| Peach | `#FF9F6B` | Second stop of the pink gradient |
| Mist | `#EEF1F8` | Text on dark backgrounds |

## Files
- `icon/`: the S on its own (blue, white, black) and the app icon (S on an Ink rounded square). `png/` has every size from 16 to 1024 px.
- `wordmark/`: the full SEND text logo in blue, white, black and Ink, as SVG and 2000 px PNG with a transparent background.
- `lockup/`: app icon and wordmark side by side, for dark and light backgrounds or transparent.
- `stripe/`: ready for Stripe, Settings, Branding:
  - Icon: `stripe-icon-512.png`
  - Logo: `stripe-logo-white-1200.png` (white, for Stripe's header in your brand colour). Turn on "Prefer logo over icon" to show it.
  - Brand colour `#FF5C8A`, accent colour `#0B1020`.
- `social/`: link preview image (1200 x 630) and a banner (1500 x 500).

## Rules
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the width of the S's top bar.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolour (outside the colours above) or add effects to the logo.
- On busy photos, use the app icon or the lockup on a solid background.
