What's inside
• Profile effects: 432 animated bundles in 18 collections, each with a profile effect, an avatar decoration, a banner and matching colours. Try any of them on your own card before you save.
• Mix and match: wear one bundle's profile effect with another's avatar decoration.
• SEND Studio: themes, glass, wallpapers and layouts, with a different look for every server and conversation.
• Screen share up to 1080p at 60 fps with sound. SEND keeps its own audio out of the stream, so nobody hears themselves back.
• Calls: pop any call out into its own window, move the tiles where you like, and AI noise suppression removes keyboards, fans and barking dogs.
• Voice servers around the world, game-only audio sharing in the Windows app, and 7TV and BTTV emotes everywhere you type.
• Sparks: complete challenges like inviting friends, joining a call with five people or going live. Each one earns 100 Sparks, and 100 Sparks buys a Server Boost that lasts a year. There are enough challenges to take a server to Level 3, with a new season every year.

SEND is completely free. If you'd like to show your support and help keep it that way, you can sponsor SEND from £1 a month in Settings, under Sponsor. It's entirely optional and keeps the servers running for everyone.
