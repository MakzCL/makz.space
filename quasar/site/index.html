What it does

Everything a peripheral suite should do,
for every brand at once.

One canvas for the whole desk

Drag your keyboard, mouse, mat, fans, strips and RAM to where they really sit. Effects are painted onto one shared surface, so a wave starts on your keyboard and carries on across your mouse, then your case. Layer effects and blend them like a photo editor.

◎

Every brand

An OpenRGB SDK client built in reaches hundreds of controllers: Corsair, SteelSeries, ASUS Aura, MSI, Gigabyte, G.Skill, Lian Li, HyperX, Logitech, Razer and more. Native USB drivers add what lighting tools leave out.

⌖

Performance tuning

Up to five DPI stages with colours, polling rates up to 8k, lift-off distance and battery level. Razer and Logitech HID++ mice are supported natively.

⌘

Remaps and macros

Rebind any key or button to keys, media, macros, DPI clutch, profile switching or launching an app. Overdrive adds a second layer of bindings while you hold a key. Record macros and edit them step by step.

It reacts

An audio visualiser listens to what your PC is playing. Screen ambience extends your monitor's colours onto the desk. Keypresses send ripples from the exact key you hit.

Game aware

Counter-Strike 2 integration, set up in one click. Health shows as a bar of light, damage flashes red, flashbangs white you out and the bomb ticks across the room.

⇄

Profiles that switch themselves

A profile holds a lighting scene plus every device's settings. Link it to cs2.exe or Code.exe and QUASAR swaps the moment that app starts, then swaps back when it closes.

⚙

Light on your PC

The engine is native Rust: one render thread, a 192×120 float canvas, and USB writes only when a colour actually changes. It runs from the tray and never makes an account or a network call.

Effect library

Sixteen effects at launch.

Compatibility

Bring what you already own.

Lighting

Any device OpenRGB supports: keyboards, mice, mats, headsets, motherboards, RAM, GPUs, coolers, fans, strips, cases. Run OpenRGB with its SDK server on, and QUASAR drives each one LED by LED.

DPI, polling and battery

Native drivers for Razer mice and keyboards (DeathAdder V2, Basilisk V3, Viper, BlackWidow V3, Huntsman V2 and more) and Logitech HID++ mice (G502 HERO, G203, PRO X Superlight, Lightspeed). The list grows with each release.

Platforms

Windows 10 and 11 first. macOS and Linux builds are published too; on Linux, remapping listens but doesn't block keys, and HID access needs a udev rule.
